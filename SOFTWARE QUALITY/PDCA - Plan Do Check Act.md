# PDCA or Plan-Do-Check-Act

PDCA or Plan-Do-Check-Act is an iterative methodology used for an continuous improvement in process, products and management systems. 

Its based in a cyclic process that is repeated continuously in the following 4 phases: 

* **Plan**: identify the problems or improvement opportunities in the development methodologies or in the developed product and define an *action plan*. 
* **Do**: implement the *action plan* in a minor scale and monitor the execution. 
* **Check**: evaluate and analyze the results from the **Do** phase and compare them with the expected results from the *action plan*. 
* **Act**: based on the results from the analysis, improvements are applied into the learnings or development.
