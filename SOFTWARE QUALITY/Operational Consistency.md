# Operational Consistency

The **operational consistency** measures the capacity of the system of maintaining a general and homogeneous pattern in the interactive elements. 

This ensures the user can predict the behavior of the elements based in previous interactions with the application. 


Metric: 
$$X=1-\frac A B$$

* A: number of operations that are inconsistent in the product from the final user point of view. 
* B: total number of operations in the product. 
* X: grade from 0 to 1 in which the exposed operations are consistent.
